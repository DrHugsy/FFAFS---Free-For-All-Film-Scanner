What this is

Doctor Iggy's is a small set of free tools (a film negative scanner, a photo editor, a LUT lab) for photography and design. It's a personal, non-commercial project, provided as-is, with no account required.

Your photos

Photos you upload are processed to generate the result you see and download. The RAW-decoding step happens on the server only long enough to convert the file and send back an image — it is not written to disk or kept afterward. Nothing you upload is stored, sold, shared, or used for anything other than showing you the result in that same session.

No warranty

These tools are provided "as is," with no guarantee that they will always be available, error-free, or produce a perfect result for every photo. Always keep your own backup of the original negatives or scans — don't rely on this site as your only copy of anything.

Acceptable use

Don't upload content you don't have the right to process, or use the service in a way that disrupts it for other people (for example, automated bulk abuse). Reasonable personal use is exactly what this was built for.
