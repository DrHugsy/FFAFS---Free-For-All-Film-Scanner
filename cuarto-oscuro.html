How to photograph a negative

  • Backlight it evenly — a lightbox, a tablet showing a plain white screen, or a bright overcast window all work.
  • Get close (macro or close-focus mode) so the negative fills as much of the frame as possible.
  • Focus manually on the film grain itself, not on the light behind it.
  • Shoot RAW if your camera can — it decodes better here than a JPG copy.
  • Turn off flash and any "scene" color correction — a plain, flat capture works best.
  • Keep the negative flat (glass or a film holder) and wipe dust off it and the lens first.
