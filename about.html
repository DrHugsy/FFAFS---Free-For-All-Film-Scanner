About Dr Iggy's

A small hub of free tools, made by one person, with no interest in your data.

What this is

Dr Iggy's is a hub of free, no-signup tools for photography and design: a film negative scanner, a Lightroom-style photo editor, a LUT lab, an image converter, a favicon generator, and a QR code generator. The name comes from Iggy, the dog from JoJo's Bizarre Adventure — not an iguana, even though that's an easy mistake to make.

Why it's free

Every tool here started as something its own maker wanted to exist: a quick way to digitize old film negatives, a photo editor that doesn't ask for a subscription, a QR generator that doesn't bury the download button behind an upsell. No accounts, no paywalls, no "free trial." If that changes for any tool, it'll be said clearly on that tool's page — not hidden in fine print.

Your photos and your privacy

Almost everything on this site runs entirely in your browser — your files never leave your device for tools like the Photo Editor, LUT Lab, Image Converter, Favicon Generator, or QR Generator. The one exception is decoding RAW camera files (.CR3, .NEF, .ARW, etc.), which needs a quick round-trip to the server because browsers can't read those formats on their own. Even then: the file is processed in memory only, converted, sent back, and discarded immediately — it's never written to disk, logged, saved, or looked at by anyone. Nothing you upload anywhere on this site is ever stored, sold, or shared. The full legal version of this is in the Terms of Service.

Who's behind it

This is a personal, non-commercial project built and maintained by one person as a hobby — not a company, not a startup, no investors to please. It'll keep growing as long as it's fun to build.
