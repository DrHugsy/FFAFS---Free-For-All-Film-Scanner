How to photograph a negative

  • Backlight it evenly (lightbox, tablet, or a bright window).
  • Get close — fill the frame with the negative.
  • Focus on the film grain, not the light behind it.
  • Shoot RAW if your camera can.
  • Flash off, no auto color correction.
  • Keep it flat and dust-free.
